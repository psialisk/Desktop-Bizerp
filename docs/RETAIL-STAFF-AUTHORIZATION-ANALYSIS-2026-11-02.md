# Retail-family staff, roles and authorization — Philippine analysis

Research date: 2 November 2026, UTC+8. Analysis only. No application code, schema, pricing, configuration, deployment or installed-app changes. Role mappings below are product recommendations, not a claim that Philippine law prescribes these exact job titles.

## Recommendation
Use one shared Staff and Authorization capability for the Retail family, with editable specialization-specific role templates. Keep job title, system capabilities, work assignment and professional qualifications distinct. A Water checker is a useful operational equivalent of a Pharmacy reviewer, but does not possess a pharmacist-equivalent statutory professional authority.

A staff profile answers who the person is; a login identifies the person acting; role permissions determine actions; branch/shift/assignment determines where and when; qualifications determine whether regulated/specialist work is permitted. Link these rather than creating a separate permission engine/foundation for every occupation.

## Current BusinessERP source findings
Eight Retail-family specializations exist: Retail Store, Water Refilling, Pharmacy, Salon/Barbershop, Laundry, Food & Beverage, Auto Repair, Services & Contracting. Retail Store also has Bookstore, Clothing, Furniture, Grocery, Hardware and Toy workflows.

The current fixed account roles are owner, manager and staff (plus platform super_admin), with one optional tenant custom access role. Permissions use none/view/edit across departments and separate checkout access. Owner role management and changes are audited; the cloud guard rereads account/role status. Existing action-specific checks provide additional controls in some workflows.

Important gaps:
- Custom role labels do not change fixed account role. A staff account with a role named Manager/Checker may still fail fixed owner/manager approval guards, even with Finance/Operations edit. Templates alone will not resolve that inconsistency.
- Broad Operations edit is not the same as permissions to receive, count, approve adjustments, release goods or approve exceptions. Baseline manager authority is broad.
- Water fleet collection review already requires independent fixed owner/manager review and source-payment evidence. Driver/helper assignment reserves people/resources but is not an assigned-driver-only API authorization boundary in the inspected service. Water custody is append-only issue/return evidence, without a dedicated checker/pending-approval stage.
- Pharmacy already separates active, unexpired recorded pharmacist authorization from ordinary role access. Owner/manager status alone cannot perform prescription review. Owner/manager currently can access prescription records; tighter purpose-based access is worth considering. Credential recording is manual owner-reviewed evidence, not demonstrated automatic PRC verification.
- Android standalone local mode uses the activated owner identity, including local actions and pharmacist authorization. It is not multi-user staff/checker parity. Cloud login permissions should not be advertised as automatically solving offline staff attribution.

## Proposed job-role equivalents
| Business | Routine staff | Checker / specialist reviewer | Manager authority to keep separate |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Retail | Cashier, sales associate, stock clerk, receiver/picker | Receiving checker / stock controller / release checker | Price overrides, returns/refunds, stock adjustments, purchasing and credit approval within explicit limits |
| Water Refilling | Cashier, washing/refilling operator, loader, driver/helper | Dispatch/custody checker; trained plant operator or designated water-quality lead for quality controls | Daily dispatch and reconciliation; collection discrepancies, losses and commercial exceptions; quality hold release only with appropriate authority/evidence |
| Pharmacy | Cashier, inventory clerk, pharmacy assistant | Registered pharmacist assigned to professional review/dispensing responsibilities | Commercial manager handles staffing, prices and purchases; being owner/manager does not confer pharmacist authority |
| Salon / Barbershop | Receptionist/cashier, junior stylist/barber, assistant | Senior stylist / service supervisor for service quality and authorized rework | Refunds, service price exceptions, schedules and commission adjustments |
| Laundry | Intake/weighing clerk, washer/dryer operator, presser/packer | QC checker and release/claim checker | Rewash/refund/damage claims, weight/price exceptions, loss settlement |
| Food & Beverage | Cashier/server, cook/barista, packer | Kitchen lead / expediter; designated food-safety lead/FSCO function as applicable | Voids/refunds, discounts, purchasing, waste approval; food-safety holds and corrective actions need assigned competent responsibility |
| Auto Repair | Service adviser, mechanic/technician, parts custodian | Lead technician / foreman / final QC inspector | Estimates, additional-work and parts exceptions, discounts, warranty/rework and financial release; customer work consent remains a separate approval |
| Services & Contracting | Coordinator, technician, installer, field worker | Team lead / technical inspector; appropriately qualified professional for work requiring one | Quotes, variations, material issues, milestone acceptance and commercial settlement; customer acceptance stays distinct |

These are reusable job templates, not mandatory one-person-per-role staffing. One competent person may hold several roles, but independent approval must still be performed by a different person where the workflow requires it. Recording two role names against the same login does not create independence.

Retail Store variants inherit the shared roles: Bookstore adds ordering/receiving checks; Clothing size/colour exchanges; Furniture order/custom-work and delivery/installation checks; Grocery lot/expiry/cold-storage checks and food-handling responsibility; Hardware measured quantity/packing/picking checks; Toys age-label/condition/returns checks. Separate templates may aid onboarding; separate authorization engines are unnecessary.

## Philippine practice and regulatory distinctions
Public vendor documentation shows practical baselines, rather than a universal Philippine role standard. [StoreHub Philippines employee management](https://www.storehub.com/ph/employee-management) describes attendance, shifts and staff performance. [StoreHub BackOffice access](https://www.storehub.com/ph/blog/15-storehub-features) describes selectable employee section access. [Qashier staff management, all markets](https://support.qashier.com/en/articles/8014677-how-to-add-edit-or-delete-staff-in-qashierhq) documents store-scoped staff, individual Staff PINs and a separate void/refund permission. These support distinct identities, store scopes and sensitive-action permissions as sensible ERP defaults; they do not prove professional-compliance features are present or absent in either vendor.

Pharmacy: [RA10918, sections30–31](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/70354) distinguishes pharmacist-only professional functions and outlet supervision categories. Keep registered pharmacist qualification and required supervision independent of a role label, business ownership or cashier permission. Ordinary OTC and pharmacist-only OTC are not interchangeable categories. Establishment licensing is separate from individual credentials; [FDA AO2024-0015](https://www.fda.gov.ph/administrative-order-no-2024-0015-prescribing-the-rules-requirements-and-procedures-in-the-application-for-license-to-operate-of-covered-health-product-establishments-with-the-food-and-drug-adm/) provides the updated licensing framework. [PRC verification](https://verification.prc.gov.ph/) supports a documented manual verification step; do not assume an available automated integration.

Water: [DOH Water Supply supplemental IRR, section9.5](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/48321) specifies training for plant operators/management and other personnel, with stated equivalent-training provisions, and assigns product-water quality responsibility. The ERP should track appropriate training and health/permit evidence rather than treating checker or manager as sufficient qualification. [Current LGU example, Calamba](https://calambacity.gov.ph/Users/Home/ViewServicesPage?createservicesId=142) also lists operator seminar and permit/testing requirements. A container checker verifies quantity and handover; an internal quality reviewer records evidence, not a fictitious laboratory result. Professional sanitary-engineering work remains distinct from ordinary station supervision.

Food/Grocery: [Food Safety Act, sections13–14](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/58856) places food safety responsibilities on operators. [NMIS regulatory circular quoting IRR Rule14a.1](https://www.nmis.gov.ph/images/pdf/laws_issuances/MC122018022_MSQAProgram.pdf) references designation/training of the FSCO. [DOH food establishment IRR](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/39219) covers food-handler health certificates, including food-handling grocery personnel. Model food-safety responsibility and applicable credentials separately from cash management; validate exact establishment requirements with its responsible authority.

Other services: [TESDA current qualification catalog](https://www.tesda.gov.ph/download/) includes Hairdressing, Barbering, Beauty Care, Automotive Servicing and other technical qualifications. [TESDA laundry training](https://e-tesda.gov.ph/course/index.php?categoryid=30) illustrates handling/sorting/washing/return competencies. These support recording relevant skills. Their existence does not establish a universal mandatory PRC-like licence for every stylist, mechanic or laundry checker. Salon medical/aesthetic procedures and specialized contracting trades may require a separate professional/regulatory assessment.

Health/prescription information is sensitive personal information; apply purpose-based access, not blanket access merely because someone supervises staff. See [NPC health-information opinion](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/AONo_2018-056.pdf). Store needed credential verification/status rather than unnecessary full employee medical records.

## Recommended authorization controls
1. Individual staff identity; optional linked employee record; separate job title, skill, employment state and login state. Drivers, contractors or external professionals can be recorded without automatically receiving system access. Distinguish acted-by, performed-by, assigned-to and approved-by in audit records.
2. Editable Cashier, Stock/Receiving, Checker, Operations Manager, Finance and specialist templates. Deny by default; permit create, review/approve, refund/void, adjust, release and export separately. A checker should not need payroll or pricing authority.
3. Tenant boundary always enforced; branch/outlet, station/till, assigned job/trip and effective shift scopes where applicable. Assignment is validated by backend actions, not only filtered UI lists. Attendance alone does not create professional authority or prove required supervision.
4. Credentials as separate records: type, issuer, verification method/reference/date, verifier, active/revoked status, and expiry where the credential actually has one. Track establishment permits separately. Business owner may grant business permissions, but cannot make an unqualified user a pharmacist or equivalent professional.
5. Independent review for stock discrepancies, fleet collections and other selected high-risk actions. Bind approval to transaction version, amount/action, reason, approver and expiry; edits invalidate the approval. Customer work consent is separate from internal manager approval.
6. Small-business policy: combine routine jobs when needed, retain each person's identity, and avoid making all routine sales require two people. For permitted commercial exceptions, use explicit owner review with a reason and audit label; never mislabel self-review as independent review or override professional qualification/safety holds.
7. Deactivation, expiry, temporary delegation and access review. Cloud actions revalidate current authority. Offline cannot promise immediate server revocation: signed limited staff authorization must be bounded by identity/role/credential validity and a defined recheck policy. A permanent device licence must not create permanent staff authorization. Preserve historical records and attribution when access is revoked.
8. Calendar reminders for credential/permit expiry, required training and scheduled reviews, routed to the responsible staff/manager. A reminder or notification never grants authority. Keep sensitive detail out of general staff notifications.

## Pros and cons
| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Keep owner/manager/staff and department none/view/edit only | Familiar, low setup effort, fewer controls for tiny shops | Broad authority; unsuitable checker separation; titles and fixed-role approval can disagree; weak specialist credential/scope expression |
| Separate hardcoded roles/permissions per specialization | Familiar industry labels and tailored screens | Duplicate rules; inconsistent fixes; difficult combined businesses and multi-role staff; job titles can be mistaken for qualifications |
| Shared action authorization plus specialization templates and credential gates | Consistent audit/revocation; safer approvals; reusable onboarding; supports multi-role and multi-outlet businesses | More policy/UI work; existing grants require careful migration; needs small-business fallback and explicit offline design |

Detailed benefits: fewer unauthorized refunds/stock adjustments; clear Water custody/collection accountability; pharmacists can review without unnecessary financial/HR powers; finance can work without patient data; release responsibility is visible in Laundry/Auto/Services.

Detailed costs: too many prompts slow checkout; rigid maker/checker policies can deadlock a one-person business; offline revocation is delayed; time/branch scopes need reliable data; credential entry alone can create false confidence unless verified. Use a small set of meaningful actions, presets and risk-based approvals rather than dozens of hardcoded job titles.

## Conclusion and suggested order (not implementation approval)
Retain one Retail-family Staff and Authorization foundation/capability. Add specialization role templates rather than a new business sub-spec or foundation for each occupation. First clarify capabilities and fixed-role/custom-role conflicts; then prioritize General Retail cashier/stock/reviewer, Water operations checker plus qualified plant supervision, and Pharmacy pharmacist/assistant/commercial separation. Follow with Salon, Laundry, Food, Auto and Services presets using the same engine. Android local multi-user identity and staff authorization are a separate required design step before claiming checker or professional-staff parity offline.
